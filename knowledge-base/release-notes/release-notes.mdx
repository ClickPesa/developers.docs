## Major Updates

**Major Update: Enhanced Page Functionality**

We're excited to introduce significant enhancements to our platform, aimed at providing you with more insightful data and seamless navigation. Here's what's new:

**Summary and Analytics on Main Pages:** We've revamped all main pages to now include comprehensive summaries and analytics. Whether you're monitoring transactions, analyzing trends, or assessing performance, you'll have access to actionable insights right at your fingertips. [Learn More](/knowledge-base/general/get-started)  
![](/images/knowledge-base/CPT2404030719-1436x14831-ezgif.com-crop.gif)

**Unified Data with Filter Functionality:** With our latest update, you can now apply search and filters once and have them automatically propagate across all page sections including summary, graphs and tables. This streamlines your workflow, saving you time and effort while ensuring consistency in your data analysis. [Learn More](/knowledge-base/general/get-started)  
![](/images/knowledge-base/CPT2404030711-1446x102-1.gif)

We're committed to continually improving your experience with ClickPesa, and these updates represent the latest milestone in our ongoing efforts to empower your business growth.

## Release Date

This release will be available to use starting April 2, 2024.
