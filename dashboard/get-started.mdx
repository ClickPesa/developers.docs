After you register and log in, the Merchant Dashboard includes a **Get Started** section in the sidebar. It guides you through setup tasks and links to external documentation where needed.

Open it at [merchant.clickpesa.com/get-started](https://merchant.clickpesa.com/get-started).

## Setup guides

| Guide | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| **Set Up Your Account Quickly** | Six-step checklist: business profile, authorised person, KYC, payment methods, settlement account, and team. See [Set Up Your Company Account](/dashboard/setup-company-account). |
| **Enable Payment Options** | Choose and activate collection methods (mobile money, cards, TanQR, and more). Links to [Collection settings](/dashboard/settings/collection). |
| **Issue a New Invoice** | Create your first e-invoice. See [E-Invoice](/dashboard/e-invoice). |

## Developer resources

**Developer Resources** opens the in-dashboard integration hub at `/get-started/developers`. It tracks API setup progress and links to:

- [Integration Overview](/home/integration-overview)
- [API Integration Setup](/application/api-application-setup)
- [Webhooks](/home/webhooks)
- [API Explorer](/api-reference)

See [Developers settings](/dashboard/settings/developers) for applications, API keys, webhooks, and IP whitelisting.

## Finance and support

| Guide | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| **For Finance & Accounting** | Export transactions and accounting workflows. See [Data Export](/dashboard/data-export/payment-export). |
| **Customer Support** | Contact options and support resources. |

## After setup

Once your account is configured, use the sidebar to collect payments ([Payment Pages](/dashboard/payment-pages), [E-Invoice](/dashboard/e-invoice), [Checkout Links](/dashboard/checkout-links)), send [payouts](/dashboard/disbursement/single-payout), and monitor [transactions](/dashboard/transactions/payments).
