## Login

After the first login you don't have to verify your email and add your phone number for receiving an OTP again. Follow the steps below for a normal login:

1. Go to [merchant.clickpesa.com](https://merchant.clickpesa.com/).
2. You will be directed to a login page.
3. Enter your Email and Password.
4. Click the "Login" button to proceed.
5. Enter the OTP sent to your phone number.

OTP is a 5 digit code sent through SMS to your phone number. Every time you sign in with your email and password you will receive an OTP on your phone which you have to enter to access your account. The OTP is always unique and expires after a couple of minutes. If the OTP is incorrect, you are not able to login and you can resend the OTP. It is also possible to send the OTP to your email. OTP is for enhanced security.

## Reset password

1. To reset your password in case you have forgotten it, click the forgot password text under the password field.
   ![](/images/knowledge-base/SCR-20240917-csdw-1.png)
2. You will be directed to a page where you can reset your password.
   - Enter your email and click submit to get a password reset link sent to your email address.
     ![](/images/knowledge-base/password-reset-enter-email.png)
   - Check your email for password reset instructions and a link.
     ![](/images/knowledge-base/password-reset-email.png)
   - You will receive an email with the button “Reset Password”. Click the reset password button.
3. You will then be directed to a page where you can enter a new password.
   ![](/images/knowledge-base/password-reset-update-password.png)
   Add your New Password and Confirm Password then click the Update Password button.
   ![](/images/knowledge-base/password-reset-password-updated.png)
