## Introduction

In today's digital landscape, safeguarding your accounts against unauthorized access is crucial. This feature enables users to effortlessly update their registered phone numbers, used for one-time passwords (OTPs), and passwords utilized for system login.

The ability to change crucial account credentials, such as phone numbers and passwords, serves as a fundamental aspect of maintaining account security. With this feature, users gain greater control over their account's integrity and accessibility. Let's explore how this feature empowers users to take charge of their account security effortlessly.

### OTP Phone number

1. Log in to your ClickPesa Merchant Dashboard.
2. Navigate to the settings tab, which is typically located at the bottom left corner of your dashboard. And then click security tab on settings page.

<Frame>
<img src="/images/knowledge-base/SCR-20240917-dalm.png" alt="Merchant Dashboard screenshot" />
</Frame>

3. Click on Security tab on settings page and then click on "Change" button under "OTP NUMBER" card. It will open a drawer

<Frame>
<img src="/images/knowledge-base/otp-1.png" alt="Otp 1" />
</Frame>

4. Enter your new OTP phone number and your password to confirm changes and submit

</Frame>
