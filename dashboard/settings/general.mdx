General settings cover your business identity and the authorised person on your account. Complete both sections as part of the [account setup checklist](/dashboard/setup-company-account).

## Business Profile

Under **Settings → General**, update:

- **Business details** — industry, description, TIN, and VAT number
- **Business type** — set during registration and displayed read-only here. To change it, email [support@clickpesa.com](mailto:support@clickpesa.com).
- **Company name** — displayed read-only after it is set. To change it, email [support@clickpesa.com](mailto:support@clickpesa.com).
- **Contact details** — phone, email, address, city, country, default language, and website

Your company name and contact details appear on ClickPesa receipts, invoices, and payment pages.

## Authorised Person

On the same page, add the authorised contact person:

- Name
- Phone number
- Email

## Tips

Set up General settings before using payment or payout features. Default language here is used for merchant and customer notifications.

## FAQ

**Do my company details appear on receipts, invoices, and payment pages?**

Yes. Your company name, email, and contact number are shown on ClickPesa receipts, invoices, and payment pages.
