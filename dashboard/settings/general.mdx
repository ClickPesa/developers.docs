General settings cover your business identity and the authorised person on your account. Complete both sections as part of the [account setup checklist](/dashboard/setup-company-account).

## Business Profile

Under **Settings → General**, update:

- **Business details** — industry, description, TIN, and VAT number
- **Business type** — set during registration and displayed read-only here. To change it, email [support@clickpesa.com](mailto:support@clickpesa.com).
- **Company name** — displayed read-only after it is set. To change it, email [support@clickpesa.com](mailto:support@clickpesa.com).
- **Contact details** — phone, email, address, city, country, and website
- **Customer support** — email and phone so customers can reach you after they pay or receive a payout

Your company name appears on ClickPesa receipts, invoices, and payment pages. Customers use **customer support** to contact you — not your account email or phone.

Choose English or Swahili for notifications under [Settings → Notifications](/dashboard/notifications).

## Authorised Person

On the same page, add the authorised contact person:

- Name
- Phone number
- Email

## Tips

Set up General settings before using payment or payout features. Add **customer support** so customers know how to reach you. Set English or Swahili under [Settings → Notifications](/dashboard/notifications). On [bulk payouts](/dashboard/disbursement/bulk-payouts), the **Language** column applies only to that recipient.

## FAQ

**Do my company details appear on receipts, invoices, and payment pages?**

Your company name does. The email and phone on those pages are your **customer support** contacts, not the account email and phone.

**Where do I set notification language?**

[Settings → Notifications](/dashboard/notifications).
