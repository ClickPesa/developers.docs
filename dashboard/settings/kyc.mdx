ClickPesa is a licensed financial institution. Before you can use the platform without transaction limits, you must complete **Know Your Customer (KYC)** verification.

<Note>
KYC is step 3 of the [account setup checklist](/dashboard/setup-company-account). Document requirements by business type are also listed in [Get Started With ClickPesa](/home/onboarding#step-2-complete-kyc).
</Note>

## How to access KYC settings

1. Log in to the [Merchant Dashboard](https://merchant.clickpesa.com).
2. Open **Settings** and select **KYC**.

The KYC tab is available to users with the **Main** or **Manager** role when KYC is enabled for your account.

## Before you start

1. Your **business type** is set during registration and shown under **Settings → General**. It cannot be changed from the dashboard, except an existing **I am not registered / Individual** account, which selects a registered type on this page. Requirements depend on the type on your account.
2. Have registration documents ready. For **Entity Information**, see [How to obtain entity information](/home/onboarding#how-to-obtain-entity-information). Sample templates and forms are available for download in the KYC page.
3. For each required **beneficial owner (UBO)**, you will upload identity documents (NIDA, Voter's ID, Driving Licence, or Passport) for directors, shareholders, partners, trustees, or other roles listed for your business type.

<Warning>
**I am not registered / Individual** is no longer offered at signup. If an existing account still has this type, select a registered business type on this page (for example **Sole Proprietor**) before uploading documents.
</Warning>

## Submission steps

1. Review required **business documents** and **UBO** sections shown for your business type.
2. Upload each required document.
3. Complete any required **forms** (for example board resolution or ClickPesa Terms and Conditions) with signatures, seal, and witness where stated.
4. If prompted, complete **KYC search payment** before your submission is reviewed.
5. Submit for compliance review.

Our compliance team reviews your submission. You receive an email when KYC is **approved** or if more information is needed. After approval, the transaction limit is removed, API access is enabled, and additional features (such as card, TanQR/Lipa Namba, and CRDB Direct Debit collections) become available.

## Restrictions before KYC approval

Until KYC is approved:

- **Transaction limit:** **TZS 100,000 total** combined across collections, payouts, deposits, and withdraws in TZS. A separate limit applies to USD activity.
- **When the limit is reached:** You can still view your account and complete KYC, but payouts, collections, and other payment actions are paused until verification is approved.
- **Card collections:** Card payment methods cannot be enabled until KYC is complete.
- **TanQR/Lipa Namba and CRDB Direct Debit:** These collection methods cannot be enabled until KYC is complete.
- **Other collection channels:** Some additional online collection channel activations also require KYC before they can be enabled.
- **API usage:** API calls, including token generation, are unavailable until KYC is approved. Creating an application or an API key is unavailable as well. You can still review the platform with [Invoices](/dashboard/e-invoice), [Payment Pages](/dashboard/payment-pages), and [BillPay](/bill-pay/bill-pay-overview).
- You receive a notification when you reach **half** of your transaction limit.
