## Introduction

This guide will walk you through the process of doing a withdrawal. With withdrawal you can withdraw any TZS or USD balance. You can use withdrawal to withdraw the fund the your [settlement account](/dashboard/settings/disbursement) that is setup.

Oversees your withdraw transactions is a crucial aspect of any business, and being able to track and review payouts is fundamental to maintaining transparency and control over your finances. Our dashboard offers a streamlined and user-friendly interface to view all your withdraws and even export this data for further analysis or record-keeping.

## How to use withdrawal

### How to configure

In order to be able to withdraw, you need to add your bank account. [Learn how to add bank account here.](/dashboard/settings/disbursement)

### How to do a withdrawal

1. From your dashboard, navigate to the left-hand side and select "Account".

<Frame>
<img src="/images/knowledge-base/withdraws-5.png" alt="Withdraws 5" />
</Frame>

2. Click the “Withdraw” button at the top right corner of the transactions page. You can only withdraw when there is a positive balance.

<Frame>
<img src="/images/knowledge-base/SCR-20240401-smjm.png" alt="Merchant Dashboard screenshot" />
</Frame>

3. If you haven’t set your settlement account yet, you will be prompted with this alert and a button to take you to the settings to add your [settlement account](/dashboard/settings/disbursement).

<Frame>
<img src="/images/knowledge-base/withdrawal-add-settlement-account.png" alt="Withdrawal Add Settlement Account" />
</Frame>

4. On the right side of your screen, a drawer with title 'Withdraw' will appear. Under the 'Amount' field, choose the currency you wish to withdraw from. Ensure you enter the amount you want to take out of your account. Then decide which settlement type you would like to use for the settlement. You can choose from ACH (EFT) and RTGS (TISS), below is an explanation of both settlement type:
   1. **ACH (EFT)**   
      The withdrawal is settled within 1 working day. Weekend days and holidays are not included.
   2. **RTGS (TISS)**   
      The withdrawal is settled within the same day. Weekend days and holidays are not included.
5. Once everything is filled in, click "Withdraw" button on the right bottom.

<Frame>
<img src="/images/knowledge-base/Screenshot-2023-10-31-at-18.35.42.png" alt="Screenshot 2023 10 31 At 18.35.42" />
</Frame>

6. You will see a summary of the transaction you are about to make once you are sure of the details click the “Withdraw” button again to complete the withdraw process. You will see a success notification, you have completed the withdraw process wait for your funds to arrive.

<Frame>
<img src="/images/knowledge-base/Screenshot-2023-10-31-at-18.36.42.png" alt="Screenshot 2023 10 31 At 18.36.42" />
</Frame>

7. OTP will be sent out to your registered phone number. You will be prompted with OTP field to confirm the payout with options to resend if OTP was not received on your phone number.
8. Once withdraw is successful initiated you will receive [notification](/dashboard/notifications) via email or merchant.

**Note**: If your settlement bank account is **GT Bank**, the withdraw will be free of charge

## **Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)**

1. **Is it possible to export withdraw transaction data from the ClickPesa Merchant Dashboard to another system?**   
   Yes, you can export all the transaction data from ClickPesa Merchant Dashboard and then import it manually within the other system if they allow for data import.
2. **Which file formats can data be exported from the ClickPesa Merchant Dashboard?**   
   ClickPesa Merchant Dashboard allows you to export data in Excel format.
3. **What is the difference between payouts and withdraws?**  
   Payouts are transfers to third parties and withdraws are transfers to your own bank account.
4. **How long does it take before withdrawal is received in my bank account?**  
   This depends on the selected Transfer type, if selected transfer type is ACH funds will be received in 1 working day and for RTGS funds will be received instant.
5. **I did a withdrawal but the transaction is not received after T+1 working days, what do I do?**
