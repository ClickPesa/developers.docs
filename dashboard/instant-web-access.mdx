## Introduction

Imagine having the power of your favorite mobile app, but without the hassle of downloads or updates—right at your fingertips. Whether you're on your smartphone or desktop, ClickPesa effortlessly transforms into a user-friendly application that mirrors the convenience of your go-to mobile apps.

It's as easy as opening an app on your phone or clicking an icon on your desktop. Our platform adapts to your device, providing instant access and a familiar interface that resembles the apps you love.

Let's explore the simplicity and efficiency of your digital world, where you install ClickPesa app once and no updates needed.

## How to Install ClickPesa

### Mobile

**Android**

1. Visit [merchant.clickpesa.com](https://merchant.clickpesa.com/) on your phone using chrome browser.
2. At the top right side of the address bar, you will find three dots icon, click it  
   ![](/images/knowledge-base/Screenshot_20231222-114118_Chrome.jpeg)
3. It will open a dropdown, at the bottom you will find Install App option  
   ![](/images/knowledge-base/Screenshot_20231222-114126_Chrome.jpeg)
4. Once clicked you will see a popup prompting you to install it  
   ![](/images/knowledge-base/Screenshot_20231222-114147_Chrome.jpeg)
5. Once installed you will find a clickpesa icon in your phone app listing, from now on you will be one click away from accessing our app.  
   ![](/images/knowledge-base/Screenshot_20231222-114157_One-UI-Home.jpeg)

**iPhone**

1. Visit [merchant.clickpesa.com](https://merchant.clickpesa.com/) on your phone using safari browser.
2. At the bottom center side, you will find share icon, click it  
   ![](/images/knowledge-base/Simulator-Screenshot-iPhone-15-Pro-Max-2023-12-22-at-12.51.43.png)
3. It will open a drawer, at the bottom you will find Add to Home Screen option  
   ![](/images/knowledge-base/Simulator-Screenshot-iPhone-15-Pro-Max-2023-12-22-at-12.52.04.png)
4. Once clicked you will another drawer with an option to change App name and Add button, click add  
   ![](/images/knowledge-base/Simulator-Screenshot-iPhone-15-Pro-Max-2023-12-22-at-12.52.11.png)
5. Once installed you will find a clickpesa icon in your phone app listing, from now on you will be one click away from accessing our app.  
   ![](/images/knowledge-base/Simulator-Screenshot-iPhone-15-Pro-Max-2023-12-22-at-12.52.21.png)
