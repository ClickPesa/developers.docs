title: "Hosted Checkout"
description: "Accept payments in your platform by redirecting customers to the ClickPesa Checkout"
---

- Quick and Ease Integration - Simply generate a hosted checkout link, share with a customer and start accepting payments instantly.
- Hassle-Free Integration - Security and updates are fully managed by ClickPesa, allowing you to focus on your business.

## How It Works

1. **Generate Hosted Checkout Link** -
 Use [Checkout Link API](/checkout/hosted-checkout/checkout-link-api) to generate a hosted checkout link

2. **Redirect Customers**  - Add the generated checkout link into your checkout, website or share it via WhatsApp and redirect customers to ClickPesa’s Checkout to make a payment

3. **Recieve Payment Confirmation**  - Receive payment updates via [Application Webhooks](/home/webhooks#application-level-webhooks), an optional [`callbackUrl`](/api-reference/collection/generate-checkout-link/generate-checkout-link) on the [Generate Checkout Link](/api-reference/collection/generate-checkout-link/generate-checkout-link) request, or by polling the [Query Payment API](/api-reference/collection/querying-for-payments/querying-for-payments). Application webhooks apply to all payment methods on the hosted page, including mobile push. Customers are redirected after payment using the Return URL on your hosted application in the dashboard.
