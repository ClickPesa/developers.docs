---
title: "Instant Web Access"
description: "Install the ClickPesa Merchant Dashboard as an app on your phone or desktop for quick access without app store downloads"
---

Install the Merchant Dashboard on your home screen or desktop for one-tap access — no app store download or manual updates required.

## Mobile

### Android

1. Visit [merchant.clickpesa.com](https://merchant.clickpesa.com/) in Chrome.
2. Tap the three-dot menu at the top right of the address bar.
   ![](/images/knowledge-base/Screenshot_20231222-114118_Chrome.jpeg)
3. Tap **Install App** at the bottom of the menu.
   ![](/images/knowledge-base/Screenshot_20231222-114126_Chrome.jpeg)
4. Confirm the install prompt.
   ![](/images/knowledge-base/Screenshot_20231222-114147_Chrome.jpeg)
5. The ClickPesa icon appears in your app list for quick access.
   ![](/images/knowledge-base/Screenshot_20231222-114157_One-UI-Home.jpeg)

### iPhone

1. Visit [merchant.clickpesa.com](https://merchant.clickpesa.com/) in Safari.
2. Tap the **Share** icon at the bottom center.
   ![](/images/knowledge-base/Simulator-Screenshot-iPhone-15-Pro-Max-2023-12-22-at-12.51.43.png)
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**.
   ![](/images/knowledge-base/Simulator-Screenshot-iPhone-15-Pro-Max-2023-12-22-at-12.52.04.png)
4. Optionally edit the app name, then tap **Add**.
   ![](/images/knowledge-base/Simulator-Screenshot-iPhone-15-Pro-Max-2023-12-22-at-12.52.11.png)
5. The ClickPesa icon appears on your home screen.
   ![](/images/knowledge-base/Simulator-Screenshot-iPhone-15-Pro-Max-2023-12-22-at-12.52.21.png)

## Desktop

### Windows, Linux, and Mac

1. Visit [merchant.clickpesa.com](https://merchant.clickpesa.com/) in Chrome.
2. Click the install icon in the address bar.
   ![](/images/knowledge-base/SCR-20231222-kpxj.png)
3. Confirm the install prompt.
   ![](/images/knowledge-base/SCR-20231222-kqci.png)
4. The ClickPesa icon appears in your desktop app list.
   ![](/images/knowledge-base/Screenshot-2023-12-22-at-12.19.44.png)
