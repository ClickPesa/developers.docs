---
title: "Instant Web Access"
description: "Install the ClickPesa Merchant Dashboard as an app on your phone or desktop for quick access without app store downloads"
---

Install the Merchant Dashboard on your home screen or desktop for one-tap access — no app store download or manual updates required.

## Mobile

### Android

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the dashboard in Chrome">
    Visit [merchant.clickpesa.com](https://merchant.clickpesa.com/) in Chrome.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the browser menu">
    Tap the three-dot menu at the top right of the address bar.

    <Frame caption="Chrome menu on Android.">
      <img src="/images/knowledge-base/Screenshot_20231222-114118_Chrome.jpeg" alt="Chrome three-dot menu on Android" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install the app">
    Tap **Install App** at the bottom of the menu.

    <Frame caption="Select **Install App**.">
      <img src="/images/knowledge-base/Screenshot_20231222-114126_Chrome.jpeg" alt="Chrome Install App option" />
    </Frame>

    <Frame caption="Confirm the install prompt.">
      <img src="/images/knowledge-base/Screenshot_20231222-114147_Chrome.jpeg" alt="Chrome install confirmation prompt" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open from your home screen">
    The ClickPesa icon appears in your app list for quick access.

    <Frame caption="ClickPesa icon on the Android home screen.">
      <img src="/images/knowledge-base/Screenshot_20231222-114157_One-UI-Home.jpeg" alt="ClickPesa app icon on Android home screen" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

### iPhone

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the dashboard in Safari">
    Visit [merchant.clickpesa.com](https://merchant.clickpesa.com/) in Safari.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Share menu">
    Tap the **Share** icon at the bottom center.

    <Frame caption="Safari Share menu on iPhone.">
      <img src="/images/knowledge-base/Simulator-Screenshot-iPhone-15-Pro-Max-2023-12-22-at-12.51.43.png" alt="Safari Share icon on iPhone" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add to Home Screen">
    Tap **Add to Home Screen**.

    <Frame caption="Select **Add to Home Screen**.">
      <img src="/images/knowledge-base/Simulator-Screenshot-iPhone-15-Pro-Max-2023-12-22-at-12.52.04.png" alt="Safari Add to Home Screen option" />
    </Frame>

    <Frame caption="Optionally edit the name, then tap **Add**.">
      <img src="/images/knowledge-base/Simulator-Screenshot-iPhone-15-Pro-Max-2023-12-22-at-12.52.11.png" alt="Edit app name before adding to home screen" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open from your home screen">
    The ClickPesa icon appears on your home screen.

    <Frame caption="ClickPesa icon on the iPhone home screen.">
      <img src="/images/knowledge-base/Simulator-Screenshot-iPhone-15-Pro-Max-2023-12-22-at-12.52.21.png" alt="ClickPesa app icon on iPhone home screen" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>
