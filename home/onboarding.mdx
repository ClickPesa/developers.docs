Registered businesses (Individual and Organizations) in Tanzania can create an account with ClickPesa.
**Registering an account is FREE**— No credit card required, No monthly or yearly fees! We charge on transaction, you make money we make money. Find our [fees and pricing](https://clickpesa.com/payment-gateway/pricing/).

## Step 1: Sign Up
- Visit [ClickPesa Dashboard](https://merchant.clickpesa.com/account-registration) and create an account.
- The signup process is quick and takes **less than 3 minutes**.
- Once signed up, you can explore and use the platform fully, but with a **transaction limit**. This limit will be removed after completing full KYC.

### Transaction limits before KYC approval

Until your KYC is approved:

- **TZS 100,000 total** — combined across collections, payouts, deposits, and withdraws in Tanzanian Shillings. This is one shared limit, not a separate cap per transaction type.
- **USD** — a separate pre-KYC limit applies to USD activity.
- **Card collections** — card payment methods are not available until KYC is approved.
- You are notified when you reach **half** of your limit.

Complete [KYC](/home/onboarding#step-2-complete-kyc) to remove transaction limits and unlock all features.

## Step 2: Complete KYC
- Log in to your account and go to **Settings → KYC**.
- Select your **business type** if not already set. Required documents and beneficial owners (UBOs) are shown in the dashboard based on your type.
- Download sample templates from the KYC page where available.
- Upload all required business documents and UBO identity documents.
- Complete any required forms (including ClickPesa Terms and Conditions) with signatures, seal, and witness where stated.
- If prompted, complete **KYC search payment** before your submission is reviewed.

See also the [KYC settings guide](/dashboard/settings/kyc).

<Note>
ClickPesa is a licensed financial institution. To open an account for your business we are mandated to perform Due Diligence through a KYC (Know Your Customer) process. We are not allowed to sign up businesses and organizations involved in the following activities: Cryptocurrency, Adult Entertainment, Medical Marijuana, Tobacco and e-Cigarettes.
</Note>

<Warning>
**I am not registered / Individual** cannot complete KYC. Register your business (for example as a Sole Proprietor) and update your business type in **Settings → KYC** before submitting documents.
</Warning>

### Document requirements by business type

Requirements below match what the Merchant Dashboard shows for each business type. **(Required)** documents must be uploaded. **(Optional)** documents apply when relevant. Each listed **UBO** requires identity documents (NIDA, Voter's ID, Driving Licence, or Passport).

<Accordion title="Limited Company">
**Business documents**
1. Extract from register / detailed information of the entity **(Required)** — provided by your registration body
2. Certificate of Registration **(Required)**
3. Regulatory License (optional)
4. Certificate from Tax Revenue Authority (TIN) **(Required)** — TIN should show the business or company name

## Step 3: KYC Approval
- Our compliance team will review your submission.
- You’ll receive an email notification once your KYC is **approved or if more information is needed**.
- After approval, the **transaction limit will be lifted**, giving you full access to the platform.
