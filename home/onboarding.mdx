Registered businesses (Individual and Organizations) in Tanzania can create an account with ClickPesa.
**Registering an account is FREE**— No credit card required, No monthly or yearly fees! We charge on transaction, you make money we make money. Find our [fees and pricing](https://clickpesa.com/payment-gateway/pricing/).

## Step 1: Sign Up
- Visit [ClickPesa Dashboard](https://merchant.clickpesa.com/account-registration) and create an account.
- The signup process is quick and takes **less than 3 minutes**.
- Once signed up, you can explore and use the platform fully, but with a **transaction limit**. This limit will be removed after completing full KYC.

### Registration walkthrough

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Enter email and password">
- Input your email and select a password for your ClickPesa merchant dashboard. To ensure a strong password is provided, a password should:
  - have at least 8 characters
  - include at least one capital letter
  - include at least one small letter
  - include at least one number
  - include at least one special character: `` !@#$%^&*()_+-=[]{};':"\|,.<>/?~ ``
- Check the box to agree with the terms of service and privacy policy.
- Click the "Register" button to proceed.

![](/images/knowledge-base/onboarding-sign-up.png)
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Email verification">
1. An email will be sent to you containing a "Verify Your Email" button.
   ![](/images/knowledge-base/onboarding-email-registration.png)
2. Click on it to confirm your email address.
   ![](/images/knowledge-base/onboarding-email-verified.png)
3. Congratulations! You are now successfully registered for the ClickPesa Merchant Dashboard.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="First time login and OTP setup">
1. After email verification log in with your email and password.
   - Go to [merchant.clickpesa.com](https://merchant.clickpesa.com/).
   - You will be directed to a login page.
   - Enter your Email and Password.
   - Click the "Login" button to proceed.
     ![](/images/knowledge-base/SCR-20240917-csdw.png)
2. Setup OTP (One Time Password). OTP is a 5 digit code send via SMS to your phone number.
   - To sign in, you will need to setup and verify your phone number.
   - To activate OTP, enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" input field.
     ![](/images/knowledge-base/SCR-20240917-ctkc.png)
   - You will receive a SMS message with the code.
   - Enter the code into the "OTP Code" field.
   - Click "Enable OTP" to set it up.
     ![](/images/knowledge-base/SCR-20240917-ctgi.png)
   - If the code expires before you use it, simply click the "Resend Code" button to receive a new OTP.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Add a business name">
After verifying your OTP during your first login, you need to enter your Business Name. Then, click the "Get Started" button to proceed.
![](/images/knowledge-base/SCR-20240917-cqcr.png)

Afterward, you will be taken to the homepage of your ClickPesa Merchant Dashboard.
![](/images/knowledge-base/SCR-20240401-oepq.png)

Once you are at the homepage of your ClickPesa Merchant Dashboard, it is highly recommended to [setup all the settings correctly](/dashboard/setup-company-account) before using your ClickPesa account.
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

### Transaction limits before KYC approval

Until your KYC is approved, transaction limits apply and some payment methods (such as card payments for collection) won't be available for your customers:

1. Payouts and Withdraws: TZS 100,000 / USD 50
2. Collection: TZS 100,000 / USD 50
3. Deposit: TZS 100,000 / USD 50

## Step 2: Complete KYC
- Log in to your account and go to the **Settings > KYC** page.
- You’ll find all KYC requirements based on your **business type**, including:
  - A list of required documents
  - Any necessary forms
  - Step-by-step instructions
- Fill out the forms and upload all documents

<Note>
ClickPesa is a licensed financial institution. To open an account for your business we are mandated to perform Due Diligence through a KYC (Know Your Customer) process. We are not allowed to sign up businesses and organizations involved in the following activities: Cryptocurrency, Adult Entertainment, Medical Marijuana, Tobacco and e-Cigarettes.
</Note>

### Document requirements by business type

<Accordion title="Limited Company">

Make sure that all your documents are current and precise to ensure an efficient registration.

## Step 3: KYC Approval
- Our compliance team will review your submission.
- You’ll receive an email notification once your KYC is **approved or if more information is needed**.
- After approval, the **transaction limit will be lifted**, giving you full access to the platform.
