## Login

After your first login you do not need to verify your email or add your phone number again. Follow the steps below for a normal login:

1. Go to [merchant.clickpesa.com](https://merchant.clickpesa.com/).
2. Enter your email and password.
3. Click the **Login** button.
4. Enter the OTP sent to your phone number.

OTP is a 5-digit code sent via SMS to your phone number. Every time you sign in with your email and password you receive an OTP that you must enter to access your account. The OTP is unique and expires after a couple of minutes. If the OTP is incorrect, you cannot log in — use **Resend OTP** to receive a new code. You can also receive the OTP by email. OTP adds an extra layer of security to your account.

## Reset Password

1. On the login page, click **Forgot password** under the password field.
   ![](/images/knowledge-base/SCR-20240917-csdw-1.png)
2. Enter your email and click submit to receive a password reset link.
   ![](/images/knowledge-base/password-reset-enter-email.png)
3. Check your email and click the **Reset Password** button.
   ![](/images/knowledge-base/password-reset-email.png)
4. Enter your new password and confirm it, then click **Update Password**.
   ![](/images/knowledge-base/password-reset-update-password.png)
   ![](/images/knowledge-base/password-reset-password-updated.png)
