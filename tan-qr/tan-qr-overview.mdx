title: "TanQR/Lipa Namba Overview"
description: "Accept payments with a QR code and Lipa Namba powered by TIPS"
---

**TanQR/Lipa Namba** gives your business one QR code and Lipa Namba for collecting payments. Customers do not need a specific app — they can scan your QR code or pay using your Lipa Namba through mobile money USSD menus and bank channels.

It is powered by **TIPS**, with all MNOs and banks integrated. Customers can pay from any mobile money wallet or bank connected to TIPS. It works for both **physical stores** and **online stores**.

<Tip>
TanQR/Lipa Namba payments are **instant**. Successful payments appear in your [Merchant Dashboard](https://merchant.clickpesa.com) and can trigger [webhook notifications](/home/webhooks) if configured.
</Tip>

## When To Use It

- **Store collections:** Display a QR code or share your Lipa Namba at your counter, kiosk, restaurant, office, or service point.
- **Online collections:** Display or share your QR code and Lipa Namba on your website, checkout page, invoice, or customer message.
- **Simple payments:** Customers enter the amount themselves before confirming payment.
- **Easy setup:** Activate it from the Merchant Dashboard and download a branded poster.

<Info>
For amount-specific or automated QR flows, contact [support@clickpesa.com](mailto:support@clickpesa.com).
</Info>

## How It Works

1. **Activate** — Enable TanQR/Lipa Namba in your [Merchant Dashboard](https://merchant.clickpesa.com) under **Settings → Collection**.
2. **Display or share** — Print your poster, show your QR code, or share your Lipa Namba.
3. **Customer pays** — The customer scans your QR code or pays using your Lipa Namba through USSD or bank channels, enters the amount, and confirms.
4. **You get paid** — The payment is recorded instantly in your ClickPesa account.
