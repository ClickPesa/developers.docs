The BillPay-Namba is a unique 4-digit identifier assigned to every ClickPesa merchant. It serves as the root reference for all BillPay Control Numbers and can also be used directly to deposit funds into the merchant's own account.

## How to Access Your BillPay-Namba
You can find your BillPay-Namba by following these steps:

- Log in to the [Merchant Dashboard](https://merchant.clickpesa.com).
- Go to Settings → Collection.
- Scroll down to the BillPay Payments Collection section under Collection Tools.
- Your 4-digit BillPay-Namba will be displayed there.

## How to Deposit Using Your BillPay-Namba
Merchants can use their own BillPay-Namba to deposit funds into their ClickPesa balance through Tanzania’s leading mobile money services such as Tigopesa, Airtel Money, Halopesa and M-Pesa, as well as CRDB SIM Banking (APP and USSD), CRDB Internet Banking and CRDB Wakalas
