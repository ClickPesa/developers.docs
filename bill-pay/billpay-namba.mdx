The **Merchant BillPay-Namba** is a unique **4-digit number** assigned to every ClickPesa merchant.
Used directly to deposit funds into your ClickPesa account and serves as the **root reference** for offline order number generation.


## How to Access Your Merchant BillPay-Namba
1. Log in to your [Dashboard](https://merchant.clickpesa.com).
2. Go to **Settings → Collection**.
3. Scroll to the **BillPay Payments Collection** section.
4. Your **4-digit BillPay-Namba** will be displayed there.


## Depositing Using Merchant BillPay-Namba
Merchants can deposit funds into their ClickPesa balance using this number via:
- **Mobile Money:** Mixx By Yas, Airtel Money, Halopesa, M-Pesa
- **CRDB Banking:** SIM Banking (APP/USSD), Internet Banking, CRDB Wakalas

Funds will reflect instantly on your ClickPesa Dashboard, and confirmation will be sent by SMS and email.

## Deposit Tracking
- **Automatic Notifications:** Receive instant updates via [Webhooks](/home/webhooks) and Web Notifications.
- **Manual Check:** In your [Dashboard](https://merchant.clickpesa.com).
